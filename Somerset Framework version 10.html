Act 1: The AI Goes Rogue (Checklist NOT Implemented)

Imagine "Marketing Marvels," a hip agency, wins the STEM campaign contract to provide the Public body STEMtastic Scotland with a new STEM campaign. After the award and eager to impress, they unleash their latest AI tool, "Buzzworthy," to generate the campaign content……….

Bias & Fairness

Buzzworthy, trained on outdated datasets, immediately churns out text and images that overwhelmingly feature boys in lab coats, with girls relegated to "helper" roles. The social media posts use language that alienates children with disabilities, and the emails promote participation in STEM activities that implicitly require access to specific, often costly, materials or out-of-school programs, inadvertently sidelining children from lower-income backgrounds who lack these resources.

Human Oversight

Buzzworthy the AI continues unchecked and without human review, generating bizarre, off-putting images for the campaign – a group of "scientists" reputedly measure the speed of light:

...and quoting and using scenes from Futurama to explain space exploration:

This lack of Human Oversight turns the campaign into a public laughingstock and engagement with the marketing campaign is at an all-time low.

Privacy

Unaware of the issues, Marketing Marvels asks Buzzworthy to add a "STEM Personality Quiz" to increase engagement linked to the campaign. Without clear guidelines, Buzzworthy starts collecting excessive data from children, including their location, Browse history, and even their personal opinions on sensitive topics like climate change and political figures, failing to implement proper data protection measures. This not only violates GDPR but also exposes the children to potential profiling and manipulation.

Training Data

Further compounding the problem, Buzzworthy, a data-hungry AI, uses the anonymized user data for research and development purposes – a clear violation of anticipated contractual terms and proper Training Data usage.

Accountability

As complaints from parents and educators flood social media about the campaign's exclusionary messages, Marketing Marvels scrambles. There's no clear Accountability structure for AI-generated content; no one knows who is responsible for fixing the mess, and STEMtastic Scotland is furious.

Explainability

When STEMtastic Scotland demands answers about why the AI-generated content disproportionately targeted boys with messages about coding and robotics, Marketing Marvels can't explain Buzzworthy’s decisions. The AI's algorithms are a black box, making it impossible to understand why it generated such problematic content, highlighting the complete lack of Explainability. STEMtastic Scotland, shocked at Marketing Marvels inability to deduce how the campaign was exclusionary in its targeting, asks for an explanation on the rationale behind the bizarre imaginary used for the campaign from the approver. Marketing Marvel's Account Manager shifts uncomfortably as they explain that there was no human oversight involved in approving the images.

Security & Robustness

Compounding the crisis, a hacker exploits a vulnerability in Buzzworthy during the campaign's rollout, injecting malicious code into the campaign emails. STEMtastic Scotland's audience is bombarded with spam, and their reputation takes a nosedive, revealing the glaring gaps in Security & Robustness.

Control

STEMtastic Scotland demand that for the remainder of the campaign, their AI tool Buzzworthy is not used only for Marketing marvels to advise that the AI functionality of their marketing tool can’t be disabled demonstrating the lack of Control STEMtastic Scotland have over Suppliers integrating AI into procured software and services.

Result: A PR disaster, a damaged client relationship, and a whole lot of wasted time and money and crucially under current contract, your only recourse is GDPR Data regulations.

Act 2: Before the Bots Break Bad: The Framework's Foundations

STEMtastic Scotland, armed with the Generative AI framework, lays down the law in their contract with Marketing Marvels:

Privacy

STEMtastic includes strict data protection clauses, requiring Marketing Marvels to demonstrate compliance with GDPR, especially for children's data. They mandate data minimization, explicit consent procedures, and robust security measures for the "STEM Personality Quiz."

Training Data - Proactive

STEMtastic, ever vigilant, includes the requirement in the contract explicitly prohibiting Marketing Marvels from using any data collected during the campaign for training their AI models. They specify that all data collected, including quiz responses and campaign interactions, must be used solely for the purpose of delivering the STEM campaign and must be deleted or returned to STEMtastic after the campaign concludes.

Bias & Fairness

STEMtastic includes the request requiring Marketing Marvels to demonstrate how they will test their AI for bias and ensure diverse representation. They specifically request evidence of testing across all protected characteristics and require Marketing Marvels to provide a detailed plan for mitigating potential biases.

Human Oversight

The contract stipulates that all AI-generated content must be reviewed and approved by human experts at Marketing Marvels. It also requires them to provide documentation explaining the AI's decision-making process and how human intervention points are integrated.

Accountability

STEMtastic includes a clause requiring Marketing Marvels to designate a senior leader accountable for the AI's outputs. This person is the point of contact for any issues and has the authority to take corrective action. The contract also mandates regular audits of the AI system and reporting on its performance.

Explainability

STEMtastic requires Marketing Marvels to use explainable AI methods and provide clear documentation on the factors influencing the AI's decisions. They also mandate that Marketing Marvels provide training to STEMtastic's team on how to understand and interpret the AI's outputs.

Security & Robustness

STEMtastic includes specific security requirements in the contract, such as encryption, access controls, and regular vulnerability assessments. They also require Marketing Marvels to maintain appropriate cyber insurance and have a robust incident response plan.

Control

STEMtastic includes explicit contractual clauses granting them the ability to demand the temporary suspension or permanent disabling of problematic AI functionalities, or modifications to AI system parameters, ensuring they maintain ultimate authority over the AI's operation and outputs within their procured services.

Act 3: AI in Recovery: The Framework's Rehabilitation Program

Now, let's see how STEMtastic Scotland manages the same challenges *with* the Generative AI framework proactively integrated into their contract. The outcome is markedly different:

Privacy Risks - Reactive

When STEMtastic discovers that the AI is collecting excessive data from children, they invoke the DPIA requirement and demand an explanation. Marketing Marvels, forced to justify their data collection practices, must either reduce the data collected or provide compelling reasons for its necessity.

Training Data - Reactive

During a routine review, STEMtastic discovers a clause in Marketing Marvels' privacy policy that suggests they may use anonymized user data for research and development purposes. Concerned that this might include data from the STEM campaign, STEMtastic immediately challenges Marketing Marvels, citing the contractual prohibition on data repurposing. Marketing Marvels, caught in their attempt to sneak in this data grab, is forced to clarify their privacy policy and confirm that no data from the STEM campaign will be used for any purpose other than delivering the campaign.

Bias & Fairness - Reactive

When a parent complains about a potentially harmful AI-generated image, STEMtastic immediately contacts the designated accountable leader at Marketing Marvels. The leader, empowered by their role, promptly investigates the issue, takes down the image, and implements measures to prevent similar incidents.

Human Oversight Risks - Reactive

When STEMtastic notices that the AI-generated social media posts are becoming repetitive and lack creativity, they challenge Marketing Marvels on the level of human oversight. Marketing Marvels, bound by the contract, must demonstrate how human experts are involved in the content creation process and take steps to increase human input.

Accountability

When complaints from parents and educators flood social media about the campaign's exclusionary messages, STEMtastic Scotland calls the dedicated contact for reporting issues at Marketing Marvels to initiate the process for addressing harmful outcomes which doesn’t avoid the harms that have already occurred, does limit them.

Explainability Risks - Reactive

When STEMtastic is puzzled by the AI's targeting strategy for the email campaign, they use their training and the provided documentation to understand the AI's decision-making process. This allows them to have a constructive dialogue with Marketing Marvels and suggest adjustments to the targeting parameters.

Security & Robustness Risks - Reactive

When a minor data breach occurs, STEMtastic invokes the incident response plan and demands a detailed report from Marketing Marvels. They use the framework's security requirements to assess the adequacy of Marketing Marvels' response and ensure appropriate measures are taken to prevent future breaches.

Control

Despite Buzzworthy's initial issues, STEMtastic Scotland, armed with their proactive contractual clauses, is able to demand and enforce the immediate suspension of the problematic AI functionality for the remainder of the campaign. This demonstrates their direct control over the supplier's integrated AI, allowing for effective containment and preventing further harm.
